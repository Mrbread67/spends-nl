Shared budgets

When you share a budget you are choosing to show your entries in it to the people you invite. Everyone in a shared budget can read, edit and delete everything in that budget. Treat an invite link like a password, because anyone holding it can join and read the full history.

If you own a shared budget you decide who is in it. If you want to leave a budget you own to the others, hand it over to another member first.

The Apple Pay feature only reads what a payment was, so it can be written into your own records for you. Decisions you make about your money remain yours.
