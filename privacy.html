Privacy policy

Last updated 24 August 2026

Spends is a budgeting app you can talk to. This page says what it does with your information, briefly and honestly.

The short version

  • Keep your budgets to yourself and your financial data stays on your iPhone. We never receive it.
  • Share a budget and that budget is uploaded so everyone in it can see it, including everything already in it.
  • Speech recognition is Apple's. For some languages it runs on Apple's servers.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, nothing sold on.
  • You can delete your account and its server data from inside the app.

Who is responsible

Spends is made by Maarten Mughal, in the Netherlands. Reach us at hello@spends.nl.

On your iPhone

Your transactions, budgets, categories and settings are stored on your device. Your own iCloud Backup may include them; that is a setting between you and Apple.

When you share a budget

  • Sharing or joining creates an anonymous account: a random identifier, no name or email.
  • The shared budget is uploaded: amounts, descriptions, dates, categories and notes. For voice entries the note is the transcript of what you said. Existing history comes along, not just new spends.
  • Everyone in the budget can read it, and can edit or delete entries. The invite link is a key: treat it like a password.
  • Hosting runs on Supabase. Requests carry your IP address and iOS version, as any server traffic does.
  • Stopping sharing hides the budget for everyone. Deleting your account removes the account and your memberships; entries you added to someone else's budget stay with that budget.

Speaking to the app

Voice recordings are transcribed by Apple's speech framework and the audio clip is deleted afterwards. For most languages that happens on your device; when your chosen language has no offline model, Apple's framework uses Apple's servers. We never receive your audio.

Your rights

Everything is in the app: your data on screen, a CSV export in Settings, and account deletion under Settings. For anything else under the GDPR, from access to erasure, write to hello@spends.nl. Complaints can go to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Spends is not aimed at children under 16. If how the app handles data changes, this page changes with it, and the date above moves.
