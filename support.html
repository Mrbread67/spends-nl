Support

Something not working, or an idea for the app? Write to hello@spends.nl and you will get a reply from the person who built it.

It helps if you mention which iPhone you have, which iOS version, and what you did just before the problem appeared.

Common questions

I logged a spend by voice and it got the amount wrong

Tap the transaction to edit it. Speech recognition is Apple's, and it does better in a quiet room and with amounts said plainly, like "twelve euros fifty".

Where did my income go?

The dashboard shows spending by default. Switch the filter at the top of the list to see income.

How do I share a budget?

Open Budgets, pick the budget, and tap Share. You get a link to send. Anyone with that link can join and see the whole budget, so only send it to people you want in it.

Someone left, or I want to stop sharing

In the same Manage screen you can remove a member, which also refreshes the invite link so the old one stops working. If you want to hand the budget to someone else, use the menu next to their name and choose Make owner.

How do I get my data out?

Settings, then Export CSV. You get a file you can open in any spreadsheet.

How do I delete my account?

Settings, then Delete account, at the bottom. It only appears if you have an account, which happens only if you have shared or joined a budget. If you own a budget other people are still in, hand it over first.

Does my data leave my phone?

Only if you share a budget. The privacy policy sets out exactly what is sent and when, including the parts worth knowing before you share an existing budget.
