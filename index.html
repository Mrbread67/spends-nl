fair questions.

what does spends cost?
you start with a free trial, so spends can prove itself first. after that it is €5 a month, about one cinema snack. cancel whenever you like. no ads, nothing sold on.
do i need an account?
no. spends works the moment you open it. only sharing a budget creates one, because sync needs somewhere to meet.
what happens when i share a budget?
that budget is uploaded so you both see it. everything already in it comes along, not just new spends. the app tells you before it happens.
does it work offline?
yes. everything lives on your iPhone. sharing needs a connection to sync, nothing else does.
which languages does the voice understand?
english and dutch work best. recognition normally happens on your device.
how do i delete my account?
settings, then delete account. it removes your data from the server. your budgets stay on your iPhone.
